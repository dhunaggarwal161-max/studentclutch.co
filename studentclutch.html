01 — About
More than a
student page.

StudentClutch.co is an independent student-focused platform created to help young people discover their professional self through meaningful experiences, opportunities, communities and initiatives.

From participating in an MUN to finding a competition, joining a workshop or creating something of your own — the idea is simple: make it easier for students to find what they can do next.
