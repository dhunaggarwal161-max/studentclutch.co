Why StudentClutch.co?

More than a conference.

StudentClutch.co is a student-focused initiative being built to help young people discover their professional selves through opportunities, experiences and community.

Our MUN is one part of that vision: a space where students can practise public speaking, research, negotiation, leadership and critical thinking in a real-world setting.

The rooms

Six committees. Six worlds.

Choose your arena, take a position and defend it with evidence.

01

UNGA

United Nations General Assembly

Addressing global governance challenges posed by Artificial Intelligence, with emphasis on equitable access and responsible development.

02

UNHRC

United Nations Human Rights Council

Protection of human rights in the digital age, with emphasis on online surveillance, censorship and freedom of expression.

03

AIPPM

All India Political Parties Meet

Electoral reforms in India, focusing on political funding, electoral transparency and the role of social media.

04

UNCSW

Commission on the Status of Women

Bridging the gender digital divide through equal access to technology, digital education and economic opportunities for women.

05

UNSC

United Nations Security Council

The evolving nature of modern warfare, with special emphasis on autonomous weapons and Artificial Intelligence.

06

UNEP

United Nations Environment Programme

Climate resilience in the Global South, focusing on climate finance, adaptation, loss and damage.
