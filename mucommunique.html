About
MUcomMUNique

MUcomMUNique is a next-generation Model United Nations experience designed to bring students into meaningful dialogue around global challenges, policy, technology and leadership.

About
Masters' Union

Masters’ Union is a premium tech and business school in Gurugram delivering industry-integrated, hands-on education. The partnership proposal highlights practitioner-led learning and programmes spanning technology, business, psychology, marketing, data science, AI, finance and economics.

Why
MUcomMUNique

Built to go beyond conventional debate, MUcomMUNique gives students a platform to engage with real-world issues, interact with diverse perspectives and develop the confidence to lead meaningful conversations.

Committees & Agendas

Explore diverse global issues across our committees.

United Nations Commission on the Status of Women (UNCSW)

Agenda: Combating Legislative and Social Barriers to Comprehensive SRHR for Women and Girls.

United Nations General Assembly (UNGA)

Agenda: Deliberation on the Role of Arms Trade and External Interference in the Fueling of Proxy Wars in the 21st Century.

United Nations Human Rights Council (UNHRC)

Agenda: Protection of Women, Children and Displaced Refugees in the Conflicted Regions of Gaza and Ukraine.

United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP)

Agenda: Discussion upon global response to accelerating climate instability with special emphasis on unconventional environmental interventions.

All India Political Parties Meet (AIPPM)

Agenda: The Rise of Deep Fake Political and AI Propaganda Redefining Truth in Indian Elections.

Lok Sabha

Agenda: Deliberation upon the Artificial Intelligence (Ethical Regulation and Citizen Safety) Bill, 2025.

International Press (IP)

Every moment and emotion is preserved by the Press Committee’s work in photography, caricature and journalism.

Mentorship & Masterclasses

Training sessions will be taken in multiple schools, offline and online, led by experienced mentors and subject matter experts. These sessions will help delegates understand committee dynamics, research effectively, develop negotiation skills, and build confidence for the conference.
