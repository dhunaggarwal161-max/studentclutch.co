Everything between the classroom and the job offer

School teaches you the subject. We help with the part nobody grades you on — figuring out what you want, and getting good enough to get it.

Career discovery

Short assessments and 1:1 conversations to help you narrow down what "after graduation" could actually look like for you.

Mentorship

Regular 1:1 sessions with people already working the job you're aiming for — not generic advice, your actual questions.

Skill sprints

Short, practical workshops on the things that actually come up: resumes, LinkedIn, interviews, spreadsheets, public speaking.

Internships & jobs

A curated board matched to your stage and interests, so you're not scrolling through thousands of postings you're not ready for.

Resume & interview lab

Line-by-line feedback on your resume and mock interviews with real hiring context, not just a checklist.

Scholarships & grants

A running list of funding you can actually qualify for, plus help putting together the application.

How it works

No long onboarding. Just four stages, and you can be at any of them right now.

1

Tell us where you're at

A quick profile — what you're studying, and what's actually confusing you about life after it.

2

Get matched

Mentors, workshops, and opportunities chosen for your stage — not a generic feed everyone else sees too.

3

Build in public

Take on small projects and workshops that leave you with something to actually show, not just a certificate.

4

Go pro

Apply to internships and jobs with a mentor, a portfolio, and a community behind you.
